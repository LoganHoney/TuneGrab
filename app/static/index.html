Suggested for you

Every song is sorted into one category, and each category gets its own mixes, so Christian, Christmas and Bath Time songs never end up in your everyday Daily Mix (and the other way round). Sorting is automatic: Christmas titles, then your artist rules, then Apple Music's genre for the song or artist. New downloads are sorted within a few minutes. The category is also written into the song's Genre, so Navidrome can browse it.

If downloads fail with "confirm you're not a bot", YouTube wants to see a signed-in browser. Use a spare Google account, since automated downloads can get an account flagged.

  1. In Chrome or Firefox, install the Get cookies.txt LOCALLY extension.
  2. Open a private/incognito window, enable the extension there, sign in at youtube.com, and play any video.
  3. Click the extension, choose Export (Netscape format), then close the private window without signing out.
  4. Upload that file here, then press Retry failed on the job.
